# Print day — D3DD coffee pad dryer

**Printer:** Bambu Lab P2S · **Nozzle:** 0.4 mm · **Material:** PETG (next to the machine) or PLA  
**Supports:** none · **Bed:** textured PEI · **0.20 mm layers · 3 walls · 15% gyroid**

Print every part as it sits in the STL.

---

## Files

| File | What | Size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `D3DD_pad_base.stl` | Drip dish, D3DD recessed, 4 inside pads | Ø90 × 28 mm |
| `D3DD_pad_holder.stl` | Hex basket for Senseo pads | Ø83 × 108 mm |

Senseo pads (~70 mm) stack in the holder. Holder inner is Ø76 mm.

---

## What changed vs the original

- No supports (the voronoi original needed them)
- Hex lattice instead of organic cells — more pad support, still airy
- Hex drain floor instead of the decorative spiral
- Basket sits on four **inside** pads (7 mm) so used pads are not in the puddle and get air underneath
- Base locates the basket (0.45 mm gap)
- D3DD recessed in the well

---

## Settings

- Layer 0.20 mm · 3 walls · 4 top / 4 bottom · 15% gyroid
- PETG 250 / 80 °C. PLA 220 / 60 °C
- No brim unless PETG lifts
- Basket: print standing. Hex walls are self-supporting

---

## Assemble

1. Put the **base** on the counter.
2. Drop the **holder** onto the four pads. It should sit snug, not rattle.
3. Stack wet Senseo pads. Drips go through the hex floor, pool around D3DD.
4. Optional: silica packet in the well under the holder.

Fit: if the holder is tight on PETG, scrape the base inner once. If it is loose, print the base at 99%.
