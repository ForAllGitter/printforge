# Print day — D3DD cardboard press

**Printer:** Bambu Lab P2S · **Nozzle:** 0.4 mm · **Material:** PETG (wet pulp) or PLA  
**Supports:** none · **Bed:** textured PEI · **0.20 mm layers · 3 walls · 25% gyroid**

Print every part as it sits in the STL (already on the bed).

---

## Files

| File | What | Orient | Size |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `D3DD_cardboard_sleeve.stl` | Open tube, side drain holes | Floor on the bed | 150 × 80 × 50 mm inside 140 × 70 |
| `D3DD_cardboard_stamp.stl` | I-beam press, D3DD recessed | Foot on the bed | 139 × 69 × 44 mm |
| `D3DD_cardboard_sieve.stl` | Hex sieve the sleeve drops into | Floor on the bed | 158.5 × 88.5 × 11 mm |
| `D3DD_cardboard_tray.stl` | Water tray, 4 L-lips | Floor on the bed | 199 × 125 × 16 mm |

---

## Settings

- Layer 0.20 mm · 3 walls · 4 top / 4 bottom · 25% gyroid
- PETG 250 / 80 °C. PLA 220 / 60 °C
- Stamp: 30–40% infill so it does not crush
- Sleeve / sieve / tray: 15–20% is enough
- No brim unless PETG lifts

---

## Assemble

1. Drop the **sieve** onto the four L-lips of the **tray**.
2. Drop the **sleeve** into the sieve (150 × 80 mm outside into the 150.5 × 80.5 mm well).
3. Fill with cardboard pulp. Press with the **stamp** (0.5 mm slide in the sleeve).
4. Water leaves through hex holes and sleeve side holes into the tray gutter.
5. Lift the sleeve, pop the briquette out.

Fit: if the sieve is tight on PETG, scrape the well once. If it is loose, print the sieve at 101%.

---

## Plate order

**Sieve + stamp** first (fast fit check). Then sleeve. Tray last.
